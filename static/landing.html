02 / FROM HAZARD TO EVIDENCE

One pipeline.
Every perspective.

Generation, execution, and review are distinct stages.
Each contributes to the trial's evidence.

01DEFINE

Make it unexpected.

Describe a measurable hazard and the expected safe behavior.

prompt → scenario
02EXECUTE

Put the policy to work.

Use the selected worker to execute the scenario and capture its trace.

scenario → telemetry
03EVALUATE

Read the response.

Assess captured metrics against the rubric and review ambiguous cases.

telemetry → finding
04INSPECT
